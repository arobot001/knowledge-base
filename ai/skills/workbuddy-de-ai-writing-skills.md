## 选型思路（个人归纳）

- **只想去痕、改一遍就交**：`humanizer` / `de-ai-writer` / `human-touch-writing`
- **中文稿专用**：`ai-text-humanizer-cn` / `qu-aiwei-zh` / `laoke-humanizer-pro`
- **需要评分或诊断报告**：`ai-humanizer-master`（0-100）/ `de-ai-flavor`（八类通病）/ `writing-style-corrector`
- **模拟读者视角挑刺**：`lingyi-copy-de-ai-human-comment`
- **PPT 场景**：`ppt-optimiz-fix` + `ppt-polish`
- **长篇/连载**：`novel-engine`（十六维审）/ `sheqing-minyi-writer`（双轴质检）
- **多语言**：`ai-text-humanizer`（10+ 语言）
